📢 KING OF KNIGHTS – MAJOR UPDATE

Hello everyone,
The Fourth Poster of KING OF KNIGHTS has officially been released!
Thank you for the support shown towards KING OF KNIGHTS and the Fourth Poster release.

I am excited to announce that KING OF KNIGHTS is planned to be available for pre-order on August 15, 2026. The book will then go through Amazon Kindle’s review process, which is typically completed within a few days.

If everything goes as planned, KING OF KNIGHTS will officially be released on September 9, 2026 and will be available for readers worldwide.

📌 The comic will be published on Amazon Kindle, and I am also working on bringing it to other platforms as well to reach a wider audience.

I will also post a reminder around August 10–12 regarding the upcoming pre-order launch.

In addition, around September 4–5, I will release a special author video discussing the journey of creating KING OF KNIGHTS, the inspiration behind the story, development updates, and my experience bringing this project to life.

This comic has been a long journey, and I am grateful to everyone who has supported, encouraged, and followed the project from the beginning. Your support means a lot to me.

The adventure is almost here.

Thank you.
